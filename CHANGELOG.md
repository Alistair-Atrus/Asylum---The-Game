# Asylum Changelog

## v0.1.1
made some changes to the reading nook puzzle, corrected an error in the 'constellations' book, improved the parser, and fixed journal corruption mechanic

## v0.1.12
Fixed karm so that it can't be cast when already at 100AP, and entities won't cast it on their first turn.
Changed rmem to have it cost 50% of the resonance pool, rather than being free.
Reworked UI: moved save and laod buttons to bottom corner and removed drop down menus.  Added floating menus with tabs for inventory and grimoire.
Items can now be held in either hand.  For example, you can hold your lantern in your left hand and the quartz in your right.  Dark areas will require the lantern to be equipped in either hand.  Your grimoire is automatically held in your left hand when in combat.

## v0.1.12.1
Added mansion theme music embedded in the HTML itself

## v0.1.13
Added scrying room.

## v0.1.14
Fixed the archive glyph message so it correctly shows 𐜄 reco instead of Ƨ rmem.
Removed a dead duplicate "read the book" trigger.
Fixed the mobile long-press on grimoire glyphs so the description box opens on touch; the press is now 0.5 seconds, matching the phone's built-in long-press vibration.
Quartz now fortifies alignment by 20 points while held in either hand.
Fortify messages: "The quartz fortifies your alignment." / "The fortification fades."
